## Data availability

All primary data are available from the public repositories and accession numbers reported in the Methods. The analysis protocols, derived result tables, figure source data, identifier-harmonization records, manifests and environment specifications supporting this study are publicly archived in Zenodo under concept DOI **https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23210221**, release `v0.1.1-submission`. Third-party raw GEO, TCGA-LIHC, ICGC-LIRI-JP and GSE202642 files are not redistributed; their source URLs, versions and checksums are recorded in the archive. The version-specific DOI will replace the concept DOI after the release is published.

## Code availability

The executable analysis, validation, figure-generation and verification code is publicly archived in Zenodo under concept DOI **https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23210221**, release `v0.1.1-submission`, and mirrored at **https://github.com/lory-Yu/HCC-paired-meta-analysis-reproducibility**. The archived release includes the HCC-only public analysis specification, environment lock files, complete derived statistical tables and a root SHA-256 integrity manifest. The version-specific DOI will replace the concept DOI after the release is published.
